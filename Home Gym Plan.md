# 🗓️ 7-Day Home Gym Plan (Dumbbells + Bodyweight)

**Time:** 30–40 mins  
**Goal:** fat loss + muscle + posture fix

---

## 🔵 Day 1 – Chest + Core

- Dumbbell floor press – 10 × 3
- Push-ups – 10 × 3
- Dumbbell fly (floor) – 10 × 3
- Plank – 40 sec × 3
- Leg raises – 12 × 3

👉 Helps tighten chest + core

---

## 🟢 Day 2 – Back + Posture (VERY IMPORTANT)

- Dumbbell rows – 10 × 3
- Reverse fly (light weight) – 12 × 3
- Deadlift (dumbbell) – 10 × 3
- Chin tucks – 10 × 3
- Wall posture hold – 2 mins

👉 This fixes your **bad posture**

---

## 🔵 Day 3 – Legs

- Squats (hold dumbbell) – 15 × 4
- Lunges – 10 each leg × 3
- Dumbbell Romanian deadlift – 12 × 3
- Calf raises – 20 × 3

---

## 🟡 Day 4 – Active Rest

- Walking 6k–8k steps
- Light stretching

---

## 🔵 Day 5 – Shoulders + Arms

- Dumbbell shoulder press – 10 × 3
- Lateral raises – 12 × 3
- Bicep curls – 10 × 3
- Tricep overhead extension – 10 × 3
- Shoulder blade squeeze – 15 × 2

---

## 🟢 Day 6 – Fat Burn + Core

- Jumping jacks – 25 × 3
- Mountain climbers – 25 × 3
- Russian twists (with dumbbell) – 20 × 3
- Plank – 45 sec × 3

---

## 🟡 Day 7 – Rest + Posture Reset

- Wall posture hold – 2 mins
- Chin tucks – 10 × 2
- Light walking

---

# ⚡ How to Use Dumbbells Properly

- Start light → focus on form
- Last 2 reps should feel hard
- Slowly increase weight every 1–2 weeks
